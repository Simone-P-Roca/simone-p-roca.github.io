Hosting Logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically collect standard server log data (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) as part of operating its hosting infrastructure. The site owner does not have access to or control over this data. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember one preference: whether you've chosen light or dark mode, and whether you've dismissed the local-storage notice. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to any server, and is not linked to your identity.

No Cookies, No Tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts.

No Forms or Accounts

This site collects no personal data you submit, because it has no contact form, newsletter sign-up, comments, or account system. The Mail link on the Contact page opens your own email client; anything you send is a direct exchange between you and the site owner, governed by ordinary email use, not by this site.

External Links

This site links to external services, including ORCID, PhilPeople, LinkedIn, Substack, Instagram, and various academic and conference websites. These are independent of this site and have their own privacy policies, which govern any data you share with them.

Your Rights

Since this site processes essentially no personal data of its own, there is very little to exercise rights over. If you have any questions about how this site handles data, contact simone.roca@iusspavia.it.
